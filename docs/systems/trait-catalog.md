# Full Trait Catalog

Every trait in the game: **552 traits across 17 categories**. This is the complete reference. For how traits
change, interact, and drive behavior, see [Crew, Traits & Loyalty](/systems/traits). For why each of these is a
living dimension and not a stat on a sheet, see [Traits: not a spreadsheet](/systems/traits#not-a-spreadsheet).

> [!NOTE]
> Every trait is a value from 0 to 100. Today a focused subset is wired to gameplay (gates, thresholds, combat,
> inference) and the rest shape backstory and character. The direction is to deepen more of them over time, so treat
> this catalog as a living document, not a finished ceiling.

## Core Personality

**Applies to:** Universal  |  **17 traits**

_Fundamental personality structure and temperament_

| Trait | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Greed | Desire for wealth and material gain. Affects willingness to take financial risks and prioritize profit over safety. |
| Caution | Risk-aversion and careful planning. High caution makes pawns avoid dangerous situations but also miss opportunities. |
| Aggression | Tendency toward violence and confrontation. High aggression makes pawns effective in combat but may escalate unnecessarily. |
| Impulsivity | Acting without thinking through consequences. Can lead to quick decisions but also costly mistakes. |
| Narcissism | Inflated sense of self-importance and entitlement. High narcissism breeds ambition but also resentment. |
| Empathy | Ability to understand and share feelings of others. Affects moral boundaries and guilt. |
| Ambition | Drive for power, status, and achievement. Fuels growth but can lead to overreach. |
| Patience | Ability to wait for the right moment. Affects long-term planning and delayed gratification. |
| Loyalty | Commitment to player and crew. The most important trait for preventing betrayal. |
| Paranoia | Distrust of others and belief in conspiracies. Can provide caution or cause problems. |
| Confidence | Belief in own abilities. Affects performance and decision-making quality. |
| Dominance | Need to be in control and assert power over others. Leadership trait or conflict source. |
| Submission | Comfort with following orders and hierarchy. Inverse of Dominance. |
| Manipulative | Skill and willingness to deceive and control others for personal gain. |
| Honesty | Commitment to truthfulness. Inverse of Manipulative, affects trust and reliability. |
| Charisma | Personal charm and ability to influence others. Key leadership and negotiation trait. |
| Intimidation | Ability to inspire fear in others. Useful for control but damages relationships. |
